Human-Centric Architecture

Built for Real People, Not Cloud Engineers

Traditional cloud storage assumes everyone understands hierarchical directory paths. MemChat replaces confusing nested folders with a mental model you already use every day.

Folders as Chat Rooms

Every Google Drive folder behaves like a WhatsApp conversation. Open a chat to see all your documents, photos, audio memos, and notes organized chronologically.

Native WhatsApp Forwarding

Whenever someone forwards you a prescription, tax document, or receipt on WhatsApp, simply tap Share > MemChat to file it directly into the right Drive folder without leaving chat.

Zero Server & 100% Private

MemChat connects directly from your phone to Google's official Drive REST API. No intermediate backend, no third-party database, and zero monthly subscriptions.
