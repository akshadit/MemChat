Why MemChat?

Built for Real People, Not Cloud Engineers

Most people give up on Google Drive because of confusing folder trees. MemChat solves this with a mental model everyone already uses every day.

Folders as Chat Rooms

Every Google Drive folder is represented as a WhatsApp chat. Open a chat to see all your documents, photos, audio memos, and notes organized chronologically.

WhatsApp Share Integration

Whenever someone forwards you a prescription, tax document, or bill on WhatsApp, just tap Share > MemChat to save it directly into the right Drive folder.

Zero Server & 100% Private

MemChat connects directly from your phone to Google's official Drive API. No third-party servers, no middleman databases, and no monthly storage fees.
